Rescue guide

Found an animal. What now?

Follow these steps in order. Staying calm is the most important thing.

⚠️

When to call a professional immediately — Foaming at the mouth, circling, unprovoked aggression, or paralysis. Do not approach.

First aid reference

While you wait for the vet

💔 Shock

  • Wrap loosely in fleece — warmth first
  • Minimum handling — one move to quiet space
  • No food, no water — vet handles fluids
  • Monitor breathing every 2 minutes

🩸 Open wound

  • Firm pressure with clean cloth 8–10 min
  • Saline or clean water only — no alcohol
  • Do not remove embedded objects
  • All bite wounds → vet, always

🌡️ Heatstroke

  • Move to shade or AC immediately
  • Cool water on paws, armpits, neck — not ice
  • No wet towel covers — use airflow
  • Vet within 30 min even if recovered

💧 Dehydration

  • Pinch neck skin — slow return = dehydrated
  • Small sips frequently, not a full bowl
  • Wet food better than dry
  • Sunken eyes or sticky gums → IV fluids

I started Secret Friends of Animals because I kept noticing animals that everyone else walked past — and I decided to stop walking past.
